Truvylo Social Bot – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Truvylo Social Bot is used to connect authorized Truvylo social media accounts and manage product-related content.

Information We Process

The service may process account identifiers, basic profile information, authorization scopes, access tokens, refresh tokens, and content required to perform authorized publishing actions.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized account, prepare content, upload drafts, and publish content when explicitly authorized.

Storage and Security

Authentication credentials and tokens are intended to be stored locally or on a secured server controlled by Truvylo. They are not sold to advertisers or unrelated third parties.
